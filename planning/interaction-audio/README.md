# Interaction feedback

Both apps package 12 original mono 22050 Hz WAV cues, generated by `tools/generate_interaction_audio.py`: tap, page, welcome, start, category, success, success2, success3, retry, celebration, unlock, train. All files are nonempty, unclipped, and byte-identical in Android assets and the iOS bundle. Runtime gain is 0.55.

Connections:
- Splash greeting/wave and sparkles; explicit start cue and press motion.
- Page transitions; category pop with card press enlargement.
- Word playback indicators, including the Word Train Listen action.
- Listening, matching, memory and Word Train answer feedback. Correct answers choose one of three short cues and show stars; incorrect choices use the gentle retry cue and shake feedback.
- Lesson/game completion: dedicated celebration cue, companion motion, short confetti, individual reward-star flights and counter arrivals.
- Newly earned companions: surprise cue and animated companion announcement. Existing unlocks do not announce again on a normal launch.
- Word Train retains its dedicated departure cue.

Playback uses the Android media/game channel and iOS playback session. It follows the app sound preference and device media volume. Quiet UI cues cannot cut off important feedback for 600 ms; ordinary taps/transitions are throttled at 180 ms. Spoken lesson audio keeps priority, and interaction effects stop in the background. Generic touch feedback remains disabled around speech recording.

Validation (2026-10-25): Android and iOS Debug builds pass. All 12 cues are present and byte-identical in both built packages. On connected Samsung SM-P617, app updated and launched, splash sparkles visually inspected; successful SoundPool stream starts recorded for welcome, start, category, page, random success3 and celebration. A real two-word matching test completed and earned 10 stars (95 → 105); UI dump captured the animated counter at 104 during arrival. Tablet media volume was initially zero and was set to 5/15 for this test. Unlock, wrong-answer and iOS runtime paths are wired and compile, but have not yet been exercised on a device in this validation.

## Reward timing and lesson replay (2026-10-25)

Each star flies for 1.6 seconds (previously 0.7), staggered by 350 ms (previously 140). Both platforms count arrivals at exactly 1.85 s + index × 0.35 s. Five stars finish within 3.75 s; ten within 5.5 s. Reduced motion still displays the final balance immediately.

Lesson rewards are persisted by language and lesson ID. A newly completed lesson grants 5 stars once; repeats grant zero, including after app restart. Previously completed lessons are seeded into the reward ledger when upgrading; existing balances are retained. Android seeds from completed lesson word sets, iOS from recorded lesson completion IDs. Only fully studied lessons can claim the reward. Game rewards remain 10 per completed round.

Validation: both builds pass, Android 144 unit tests pass, Swift core checks pass. Checks cover first completion, repeated completion, persistence/reload, language isolation and already-completed legacy lessons.

## Companion destination and matching result (2026-10-25)

The unlock announcement now offers “Arkadaşlarıma git” / “Go to my friends”, opening a full-screen companion picker with a Close action. Selection reuses the existing persisted companion flow; it does not reset the current activity.

Matching now replaces its play board with a dedicated result screen after a 750 ms final-match hold (immediate with reduced motion). Result content contains the reward celebration, first-answer score, replay, family share, change-level and back-to-games controls. Android uses the common completed-game screen; iOS switches to its result branch and resets scroll position.

Validation: Android Debug and iOS simulator Debug builds pass.

## iOS action alignment and timing (2026-10-26)

The common iOS action style now centers a capped surface (280 pt on regular-width screens, 320 pt on compact screens). Completion actions for lessons, memory and matching inherit the same layout, along with other shared action buttons. Explicit trailing alignment on the home lesson action and lesson Next action was removed. Bordered category discovery also uses the centered action bounds.

Only iOS reward timing changed: 1.2-second flights with a 250 ms stagger; the counter advances at each arrival (1.4 s + index × 0.25 s). Android retains 1.6-second flights and 350 ms staggering.
