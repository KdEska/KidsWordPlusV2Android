# Word Train departure

Both platforms now play the same original 4.8-second steam-train effect (whistle, steam and accelerating wheel pulses) when the final word is completed. After a 180 ms layout settling interval, the full train follows the existing track off the board over 4.8 seconds, then the completion actions appear.

The general end-of-round celebration is removed from this transition so it does not compete with the train. Per-answer success feedback and persisted game credit remain unchanged. Playback is once per round, respects Sound, stops on leaving/background, and a new round resets the departure state. Reduced motion skips the travel animation. Android uses the effective LocalKidMotion setting, including system reduced motion.

The WAV is generated by `tools/generate_interaction_audio.py`, is byte-identical across apps, and has been checked for duration and absence of clipping. It is below the SoundPool decoded-size limit. Device listening/animation review remains pending because no test device is connected.
